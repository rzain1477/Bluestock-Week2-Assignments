# FinTech Research Report

## 1. Introduction

Financial Technology (FinTech) uses technology to improve financial services. It helps people and businesses make payments, transfer money, access banking services, and manage investments digitally.

## 2. Major FinTech Applications

- **Digital Payments:** UPI, mobile wallets, and online payments.
- **Digital Banking:** Online account management and fund transfers.
- **Investment Platforms:** Apps that help users invest in stocks and mutual funds.
- **Lending Technology:** Digital loan applications and credit assessment.
- **Data Analytics:** Helps financial companies understand customers, risks, and business performance.

## 3. Role of Data Analytics in FinTech

Data analytics helps financial companies identify trends, understand customer behaviour, detect unusual transactions, and make informed decisions. Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI are useful tools for analysing financial data.

## 4. Benefits

- Faster financial transactions.
- Convenient access to financial services.
- Better data-driven decisions.
- Improved customer experience.
- More efficient business operations.

## 5. Challenges

- Cybersecurity and data privacy risks.
- Online fraud and scams.
- Technology failures.
- Digital literacy gaps.
- Regulatory compliance requirements.

## 6. Future Opportunities

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, and real-time analytics are creating new opportunities in FinTech. Data analysts can help financial organisations interpret data, measure performance, and support business decisions.

## 7. Conclusion

FinTech is changing how financial services are delivered. Data analytics plays an important role in improving efficiency, identifying risks, and supporting informed decisions.

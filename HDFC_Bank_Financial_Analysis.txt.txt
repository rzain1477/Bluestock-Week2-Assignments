# HDFC Bank — Financial Statement Analysis

**Company:** HDFC Bank Limited  
**Financial Year:** 2025–26  
**Purpose:** Bluestock Week 2 Prerequisite Assignment

## 1. Introduction

HDFC Bank Limited is one of India's major private-sector banks. It accepts deposits, provides loans, offers payment services, and earns income from banking operations.

## 2. Key Financial Figures

| Financial Indicator | FY 2025–26 |
|---|---:|
| Net Revenue | ₹1,91,218.6 crore |
| Profit After Tax | ₹74,671.3 crore |
| Total Balance Sheet Size | ₹43,64,886 crore |
| Total Deposits | ₹31,05,251 crore |
| Advances | ₹29,37,166 crore |
| Net Interest Margin | 3.34% |

Source: HDFC Bank Integrated Annual Report 2025–26.

## 3. Profit and Loss Analysis

The bank reported net revenue of ₹1,91,218.6 crore and profit after tax of ₹74,671.3 crore. Profit after tax increased by approximately 10.9% compared with FY 2024–25.

This indicates an increase in reported annual profit. Analysts should also examine interest expenses, operating costs, loan-loss provisions, and asset quality to understand the reasons behind the change.

## 4. Balance Sheet Analysis

The bank's total balance sheet size increased by 11.6% to ₹43,64,886 crore. Deposits increased by 14.4% to ₹31,05,251 crore, while advances increased by 12.1% to ₹29,37,166 crore.

Deposits represent money placed by customers with the bank. Advances represent loans and other credit extended by the bank. Both are important indicators of banking activity.

## 5. Cash Flow Statement

A bank's cash flow statement reports cash movements from operating, investing, and financing activities. These figures help analysts understand liquidity and cash management.

The exact cash flow figures should be taken from the official annual report before drawing conclusions.

## 6. Important Banking Ratio

Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 3.34% in FY 2025–26. NIM measures the difference between interest earned on interest-earning assets and interest paid on borrowings and deposits, relative to average earning assets under the applicable reporting methodology.

Other important measures include Return on Assets (ROA), Return on Equity (ROE), Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA), Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA), and Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR).

## 7. Conclusion

HDFC Bank reported higher annual profit, growth in deposits, and growth in advances during FY 2025–26. These figures provide useful information about its business scale and performance.

A complete assessment should also consider operating expenses, credit quality, capital adequacy, cash flows, and comparisons with previous years.
